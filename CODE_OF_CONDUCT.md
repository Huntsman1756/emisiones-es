# Code of Conduct

Emisiones ES is a public research and engineering repository. Participation is expected to remain professional, evidence-based, and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss technical and research questions on their merits.
- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give and receive constructive criticism without personal attacks.
- Preserve the project's auditability: do not pressure contributors to hide, rewrite, or soften negative experimental results.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal or confidential information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, sexualized or derogatory language, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other spaces used to collaborate on Emisiones ES, including situations where someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary project moderation, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile associated with this repository. If a report contains sensitive or private information, use a private GitHub Security Advisory rather than a public issue.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or block users when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Reports will be handled with discretion and with attention to the available evidence.
